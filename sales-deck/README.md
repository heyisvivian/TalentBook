# TalentBook sales deck

A 10-slide English sales deck for Canadian engineering and construction recruitment agencies (5–30 people).

The editable deck lives as a Claude Slides artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/9SZdmqitJbMywkNYMPLTCo. It is private until shared from its Share menu, and it exports to PowerPoint or PDF from Share › Export.

This folder keeps the deck's source so it is versioned with the product:

- `deck.json`: title, slide order, sections and typefaces (Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree from Google Fonts)
- `slides/<id>.html`: one 1920×1080 slide per file, inline styles only, with speaker notes in the `<aside>`

The slides use the Slides format's own elements (`<x-icon>`), so they are meant to be opened in the Slides artifact, not directly in a browser.

## Slides

| # | Slide | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | cover | Find every engineer you’ve ever spoken to. In one sentence. |
| 2 | statement | Your next placement is probably already in your database. |
| 3 | pains | Search misses the notes, follow-ups slip, new Ontario posting rules |
| 4 | search | Natural-language search, with how the query was read |
| 5 | profile | Summary, memory cards, contact history, matching jobs |
| 6 | rules | Canadian rules checked as you work |
| 7 | engineering | Licences, work permits, engineering skills, match score |
| 8 | fees | Clean client copy, need-to-know access, three money views, guarantees |
| 9 | compare | Compared with a typical recruitment CRM |
| 10 | pilot | 30-day pilot and contact details |

## Before sending

- Fill in `[Your name]`, `[email]`, `[phone]` and `[price]` on slide 10.
- Check that the demo link `heyisvivian.github.io/TalentBook` opens.
- The deck has no statistics or customer quotes. Add real pilot results when you have them.
